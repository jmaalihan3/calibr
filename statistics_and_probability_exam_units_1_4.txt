UNIT 1: ANALYZING CATEGORICAL DATA
======================================================================

Question 1
A researcher classifies individuals into three eye color groups: Blue, Brown, and Green. What type of variable is eye color?
  A) Quantitative discrete
  B) Quantitative continuous
  C) Categorical nominal
  D) Categorical ordinal

Question 2
A conditional relative frequency distribution is used primarily to examine which of the following?
  A) The overall proportion of each category in a single variable regardless of other variables.
  B) The distribution of one categorical variable among a specific subgroup defined by another categorical variable.
  C) The shape and spread of a quantitative dataset.
  D) The standard deviation across multiple sample groups.

Question 3
Which of the following visual displays is most appropriate for comparing proportions across categories within two or more groups?
  A) Histogram
  B) Stem-and-leaf plot
  C) Segmented bar chart
  D) Boxplot

Question 4
In a two-way table comparing gender (Row: Male, Female) and preferred leisure activity (Column: Sports, Reading, Gaming), what is a marginal distribution?
  A) The distribution of preferred leisure activity calculated only for females.
  B) The distribution of gender calculated only for respondents who prefer gaming.
  C) The distribution of one variable alone, using the total row or column counts divided by the grand total.
  D) The proportion of respondents who are both male and prefer sports.

Question 5
If two categorical variables are independent, what would you expect to see when comparing conditional relative frequencies?
  A) The conditional distributions of one variable differ significantly across the categories of the second variable.
  B) The conditional distributions of one variable are nearly identical across all categories of the second variable.
  C) The joint relative frequencies must all equal 0.50.
  D) The total counts for each row and column must be equal.

Question 11
Which set of summary statistics is preferred when describing a dataset that is strongly skewed or contains extreme outliers?
  A) Mean and standard deviation
  B) Median and interquartile range (IQR)
  C) Mean and range
  D) Variance and standard error

Question 12
A dataset has a minimum value of 12, Q1 of 25, median of 34, Q3 of 46, and maximum value of 85. According to the standard $1.5 \times \text{IQR}$ rule, which of the following boundaries determines whether a high value is an outlier?
  A) Any value greater than 67.5
  B) Any value greater than 77.5
  C) Any value greater than 80.0
  D) Any value greater than 85.0

Question 13
If a constant value of 5 is added to every score in a quantitative distribution, how are the mean and standard deviation affected?
  A) The mean increases by 5, and the standard deviation increases by 5.
  B) The mean increases by 5, but the standard deviation remains unchanged.
  C) The mean remains unchanged, but the standard deviation increases by 5.
  D) Both the mean and standard deviation remain unchanged.

Question 14
What does the z-score of an observation measure?
  A) The probability that the observation occurs by random chance.
  B) The number of standard deviations an observation falls above or below the mean.
  C) The percentage of observations that fall below that value in a normal distribution.
  D) The distance between the observation and the median in units of IQR.

Question 15
If a student scores in the 85th percentile on a standardized test, what does this mean?
  A) The student answered 85% of the questions correctly.
  B) Approximately 85% of all test takers scored equal to or lower than this student.
  C) The student's score is 1.85 standard deviations above the mean score.
  D) Only 85 students scored higher than this student.

Question 17
A standard normal distribution (Z-distribution) has a mean ($\mu$) and standard deviation ($\sigma$) of:
  A) $\mu = 0, \sigma = 1$
  B) $\mu = 1, \sigma = 0$
  C) $\mu = 100, \sigma = 15$
  D) $\mu = 50, \sigma = 10$

Question 18
Using the 68-95-99.7 (Empirical) Rule, approximately what percentage of observations in a normal distribution lie between $z = -2$ and $z = +2$?
  A) 34%
  B) 68%
  C) 95%
  D) 99.7%

Question 19
Heights of adult women in a city are approximately normally distributed with a mean of 65 inches and a standard deviation of 2.5 inches. What is the z-score of a woman who is 70 inches tall?
  A) $+1.0$
  B) $+1.5$
  C) $+2.0$
  D) $+2.5$

Question 20
A normal probability plot is constructed to assess whether a dataset comes from a normally distributed population. What visual pattern indicates that the data is approximately normal?
  A) Points forming a clear U-shaped curve
  B) Points roughly following a straight linear line
  C) Points clustered heavily at both ends with a gap in the middle
  D) Points scattered completely randomly with no discernible trend
